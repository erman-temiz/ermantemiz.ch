Services

Three ways I move your project forward

I work where technology, deadlines and people meet: between engineering, purchasing, production and the customer. You get a project manager who can read drawings and keeps an eye on the critical path.

L-01 · INTERIM

Interim project management

I take full responsibility for an ongoing or new project during a vacancy, illness or overload.

  • Project leadership from order clarification to acceptance
  • Control of cost, schedule and quality
  • Customer and supplier communication
  • Claim and change management
ResultThe project keeps running without internal reshuffling.
L-02 · PLANNING

Scheduling & project coordination

Reliable plans and a clear status, so your management can decide instead of chasing updates.

  • Schedules with critical path in MS Project
  • Milestone and resource planning
  • Plan-vs-actual tracking and status reports
  • Coordination of trades and interfaces
ResultEveryone on the project knows what needs to be done by when.
L-03 · QUALITY

Technical approvals & process quality

Methods from the automotive industry for clean documents and stable processes.

  • Review and approval of CAD drawings
  • Document control and revision status
  • FMEA, 8D, A3 and process audits to VDA 6.3
  • Supplier evaluation and failure analysis
ResultLess rework on site and at the customer.

FAQ

Good to know

What clients usually ask before the first conversation.

How quickly can you start?

For part-time assignments, usually within one to two weeks of the order.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. Especially in the start-up phase and for site or production appointments, I'm happy to be on site. Status tracking and planning work well remotely.

How is billing done?

Hourly or at a daily rate with a monthly timesheet, or as a fixed price for clearly defined work packages.

Do you also take on projects in Germany and Austria?

Yes. I worked in Germany for many years and know how things work there. Remotely or with agreed on-site days.
